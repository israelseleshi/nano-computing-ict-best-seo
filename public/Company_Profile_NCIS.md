## 05. Services & Technical Capabilities

*Comprehensive security engineering, building automation, and converged IT infrastructure solutions.*

### CCTV, Security System

We provide all types of security systems to help businesses as well as homeowners manage and protect their premises. Security systems provide detection of any intrusion into an area or building. Some of our systems are standalone, but the majority are integrated with CCTV, Access Control, or Building Management Systems. Whether a small corner shop or a corporate headquarters, we deliver high-performance IP and HD camera arrays with full remote mobile access and reliable NVR recording storage.

### Door Access Control & Time Attendance Systems

There are different types of door access control systems available in the market: biometric fingerprint recognition, RFID smart cards, digital keypad readers, and electric magnetic strike locks. Access control systems restrict and log access to protected zones. Combined with our time and attendance logging software, businesses can automatically track employee attendance, shift timings, and overtime records with seamless Amharic and English localization.

### Mobile App Development

We provide end-to-end cross-platform and native mobile application engineering for iOS and Android. Perfect for startups and enterprises seeking rapid deployment with shared logic (React Native, Flutter) or high-performance native implementations (Swift, Kotlin). We handle full-cycle architecture: UI/UX design, REST API integrations, offline synchronization, push notification servers, and App Store / Google Play Store publication.

### Web App Development

We provide web application services across modern technology stacks: PHP (Laravel, CodeIgniter), JavaScript/TypeScript (React, Next.js, Node.js, Angular), responsive CSS frameworks (Tailwind, Bootstrap), and enterprise content management systems (WordPress, custom portals). From robust corporate websites and customer self-service portals to complex ERP backends, our code is engineered for security, speed, and long-term scalability.

### Computer Network Design and Installation

A strong network is a critical foundation for modern business operations. How well your network performs affects everything from file sharing and VoIP telephony to database responsiveness. We plan and build business-grade structured cabling (Cat6/Cat6A), optical fiber backbones, server rack patch panels, core routing, VLAN switching, and enterprise firewalls that keep workflows flowing smoothly and corporate data secure.

### Server Solution

Nano Computing and ICT provides a full range of server solutions for the demands of enterprise environments. From high performance servers engineered for the enterprise with advanced reliability, availability, and serviceability features to virtualization hypervisors (VMware, Proxmox), active directory domain controllers, and automated scheduled backup arrays designed for scalability, performance, and power efficiency.

### Sound System Solutions

Experience has taught us that the best type of multi-room audio system has to be seamlessly installed, good to look at, and provide high quality sound. Our service includes Building Automation Systems, Virtual / Interactive Classrooms, Professional Integration of Audio-Visual & Security Solutions, Public Address (PA) systems, background music zoning, and Digital Conference Systems offering immersive high-fidelity audio and video.

### WiFi Solution

Nano Computing presents high-density wireless LAN and campus-wide Wi-Fi zone setups. Wireless networks provide the easiest, most flexible alternative to traditional wired drops for expanding workforces. Our portfolio includes Point-to-Point connectivity, Point-to-Multipoint distribution, seamless roaming controller setups, and large-campus mesh coverage engineered to handle heavy mobile device density with enterprise WPA3 encryption.

### Disaster Recovery

Is your hard drive unable to boot? Is it knocking, or now inaccessible? We specialize in professional hard drive data recovery and file restoration across mechanical HDDs, solid-state drives (SSDs), flash memory, and RAID arrays. We handle critical recoveries resulting from hardware mechanical failures, power surges, water damage, file corruption, and accidental deletion, all under strict confidentiality protocols.

### Computer Repair & Apple Computer Service

We offer expert component-level hardware repair and maintenance for residential, commercial, and diplomatic clients. We are one of the premier local support specialists for Apple computers (MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, iMac, Mac mini). Services include motherboard diagnostics, micro-soldering, liquid spill treatment, cracked screen replacement, battery replacement, thermal overhaul, and operating system restoration.
